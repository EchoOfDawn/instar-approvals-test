One-time setup

This page has already made the verifier's signing key, in this browser only. It is sent nowhere.

1. Tap below: it copies the key and opens GitHub's secret form. Type the name VERIFIER_SIGNING_KEY, paste the key as the value, and tap "Add secret".

Copy key and open the secret form

2. Save the installation record (it holds only the public half): tap "Commit changes...", then "Commit changes".

Save installation record

3. Protect the record: download the protection ruleset, open Rulesets, tap "New ruleset", then "Import a ruleset", choose the downloaded file and tap "Create". It stops anyone deleting or rewriting the history of your approvals; the verifier refuses to work without it.

Download the protection ruleset Open Rulesets

4. Add your passkeys, on your phone and on your Mac, before relying on approvals: open this page with #enrol on each device.

    If you lose your phone

    Nothing is locked. Add a passkey on your phone and on your Mac before you rely on approvals, so either one still works; Apple passkeys also sync through iCloud Keychain.

    If every passkey is lost: from any computer signed into your GitHub account (GitHub has its own recovery codes), open this page with #enrol, add a new passkey and save it. To retire a lost device, delete its file in the approvers folder of your approvals repository on GitHub.

    A missing passkey only pauses the changes that need your approval. The agent keeps running within its limits, and "stop" in Telegram keeps working.
